# Research brief

## Objective
For independently specified crop–location–decision-date cases anchored in the Ames area, does an agentic GeoAI workflow produce more correct, evidence-supported Federal crop insurance shortlists than text-only retrieval or a fixed GIS-plus-rules workflow?

## Scope
Investigate feasibility, competing research, authoritative insurance sources, and a falsifiable benchmark. Corn and soybeans are proposed development scope, subject to human confirmation. Ames-area locations must be independently verified; do not assume every case is in one county. Do not predict losses, rank financial suitability, or recommend insurance purchases.

## Comparisons
Primary: adaptive single-agent workflow versus competent frozen GIS-plus-rules workflow with identical permitted documents and GIS tools. Secondary: text-only retrieval as a spatial-information ablation. Match model/version where language models are used, source snapshots, budgets, and development effort; log runtime and cost.

## Research questions
Which crop/year/location/date rules are actually accessible? When is GIS necessary? Which existing benchmarks challenge a generic novelty claim? What independent annotations and uncertainty analysis would permit a fair comparison? What result would defeat useful improvement?

## Budget and stopping
Per investigation: at most 6 research search queries, 8 source retrieval attempts, and 4 retained papers. Record failures. Product documentation and publication verification are operational checks, logged separately. Stop when sufficient evidence supports a bounded next step or the budget is exhausted. Do not contact people, purchase data, or expand scope without discussion.

## Output
Preserve unique runs containing activity_log.md, literature_evidence.md, gap_assessment.md, study_protocol.md, feasibility_matrix.csv, and a 500–650 word briefing.md. Publish a self-contained briefing at reports/latest.md with date, run ID, and verification phrase. No empirical success claim without independent cases and executed comparisons.

## Initial candidate and counterevidence
The candidate contribution is reliable, temporally grounded insurance screening using adaptive spatial reasoning. This framing is supplied by the agent from the user's question, not represented as a prior human-authored novelty statement. It would be weakened if competent fixed rules perform equally well, most cases need only county lookup, or added adaptive execution increases unsupported eligibility claims.
