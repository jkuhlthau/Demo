## P01
Abhinav Pothuri, Zhe Jiang, Zelin Xu, Di Yang (2026). *GISAgentBench: A Practitioner-Sourced Benchmark for Evaluating LLM Agents on GIS Tasks*. arXiv preprint v1, submitted 3 August 2026. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01645); DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2608.01645. Access: abstract and metadata only; support: Abstract.

The authors describe practitioner-derived GIS tasks with executable references and tolerance-aware output checking. This is competing work for generic GIS-agent benchmarking. Exact case construction, tolerances, leakage controls, and applicability to insurance remain unverified. No Ames insurance finding is established.

## P02
Bo Yu, Cheng Yang, Dongyang Hou, Chengfu Liu, Jiayao Liu, Chi Wang, Zhiming Zhang, Haifeng Li, Wentao Yang (2026). *GeoAgentBench: A Dynamic Execution Benchmark for Tool-Augmented Agents in Spatial Analysis*. arXiv preprint v1, submitted 15 April 2026. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13888); DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2604.13888. Access: abstract and metadata only; support: Abstract.

The abstract describes a GIS execution environment, parameter-oriented scoring, and a reactive agent architecture. Detailed metrics and comparative design were not inspected. It challenges novelty based only on adaptive tool execution; no insurance-domain validation is established.

## P03
Yuxing Chen, Weijie Wang, Sylvain Lobry, Camille Kurtz (2024). *An LLM Agent for Automatic Geospatial Data Analysis*. arXiv preprint v2, revised 25 October 2024. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18792); DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2410.18792. Access: abstract and metadata only; support: Abstract.

The abstract describes GeoAgent combining retrieval, code execution, static analysis, and search-based planning for geospatial tasks. Its reported gains motivate comparison but do not transfer to crop insurance. Baseline competence, exact scoring, and full validation remain unknown from this inspection.

## O01: Insurance plans

## O02: Insurance cycle
