# Candidate contribution and feasibility

## Evidence
P01–P03 already address GIS agents or their evaluation. O01–O03 show that insurance screening requires operative product/location/time rules, and that a usable-looking interface does not provide case evidence by itself. L01 documents missing independent labels and unverified spatial inputs. See literature_evidence.md for honest access levels.

## Inference
The strongest candidate contribution is an auditable insurance-screening benchmark with independent labels, temporal source control, clause-supported shortlists, and meaningful abstention. A domain application alone does not establish novelty; full-method comparisons and a broader search are still required.

## Competing explanations
Text-only retrieval may lose because it lacks spatial information. A fixed workflow may match the agent because most cases need simple county lookup. Adaptive retries might increase cost without correctness. Greater abstention might make answered cases look safer while reducing usefulness. All are plausible and untested.

## Feasible first step
Obtain one independently authored and adjudicated crop/location/date case, versioned actuarial rows and provisions, verified geometry, and a documented answer set. Establish why each spatial operation matters. If that case cannot be supported, stop at an access/feasibility finding rather than inventing eligibility.

## What could invalidate the contribution
No sufficient independent case families; no reliable historical source snapshots; no defensible spatially demanding insurance decisions; competent fixed rules with equivalent accuracy and lower cost; or useful-gain estimates inconsistent with the frozen criterion.

## Unresolved decisions
Crop/year/product scope, case geography, expert review access, independent group count, matched-coverage procedure, minimum useful gain, false-eligibility tolerance, and resource budget. These are human design decisions before testing. No novelty, causality, policy relevance, or transfer claim is approved.
